Every major release cycle spawns exaggerated claims about hyper-realistic police AI. We separated corroborated engine patents from forum wishlists.

## What Is Substantiated

Patent filings covering dynamic dispatch routing show NPC units establishing visual cones and containment perimeters rather than spawning directly behind a player's camera frustum.

## What Remains Unverified

Reports that law enforcement permanently logs vehicle VINs across multi-day in-game cycles have **no primary-source confirmation**. Until demonstrated in official gameplay capture, treat persistent plate blacklists as unverified.
