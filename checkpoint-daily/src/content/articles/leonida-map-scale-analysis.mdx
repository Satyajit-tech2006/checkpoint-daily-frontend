Open-world scale is meaningless without density. While Vice City's Ocean Drive neon strip dominates early headlines, our frame-by-frame geographical triangulation reveals three distinct regional hubs across the state of Leonida.

## 1. The Vice-Dale Urban Corridor

Downtown Vice City anchors the eastern coastline, connected via multi-lane causeways to Venetian-style barrier islands and an expanded international airport terminal. Unlike earlier iterations that treated waterways as decorative borders, deep-water marinas and cargo channels cut directly through commercial districts.

## 2. Grassrivers & The Southern Keys

South of the metro sprawl, highway infrastructure narrows into two-lane causeways threading through mangrove wetlands and airboat channels. Expect dynamic weather visibility and off-road traversal to play a far larger tactical role outside city limits.

## 3. Port Gellhorn's Industrial Belt

On the western coast, strip malls, pawn brokers, and rusted shipping yards form a stark economic counterweight to Vice City's luxury high-rises—setting the stage for turf friction between rival crews.
