## Background & Operational Profile

Emerging from correctional custody in Leonida with a clear-eyed view of how Vice City rewards ruthlessness, Lucia Caminos prioritizes leverage and precision over reckless improvisation. Rather than chasing street-level notoriety, she targets high-margin scores where timing and insider intelligence minimize heat.
